FAQ

Before you book a fit call.

What happens in the free 15-minute fit call?

We discuss one business problem, decide whether there is a fit, and identify whether diagnosis or implementation is the sensible next step.

Is consulting separate from implementation?

Yes. Diagnosis and recommendations can be delivered as a separate consulting engagement. Implementation receives its own scope and quote.

Do you already operate a live chatbot or Voice AI service?

The gallery currently shows clearly labelled prototypes and illustrative workflows. A live deployment is scoped, tested, and approved for each business.

Can you work with our current tools?

Yes. Diagnosis identifies what should stay, what should connect, and where a platform change or custom build is justified.

What does chatbot or voice-receptionist customization include?

Approved answers, business hours, intake questions, booking or call routing, and human escalation are defined around your operation.

What happens after delivery?

The agreed system is reviewed, documented, and handed off. Measurement and ongoing care can be included when useful.
