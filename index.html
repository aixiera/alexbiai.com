FAQ

Questions local owners actually ask.

Where can AI help in my business?

Useful starting points include sorting intake, extracting information, drafting routine responses, and directing exceptions to a person.

Can you connect the tools we already use?

Yes. The checkup identifies what should stay, what should connect, and where a clearer handoff can save time.

How can you improve our CRM or accounting workflow?

I can improve intake, data handoffs, reminders, and reporting around your core business systems.

What kinds of tasks can be automated?

Lead routing, acknowledgements, reminders, status updates, document handling, customer intake, notifications, and routine reporting are common starting points.

Can you rebuild an existing website?

Yes. The first step is deciding whether the current site needs a focused conversion improvement or a full rebuild.

How long does a small project take?

Timing depends on access, integrations, and scope. After the checkup, you receive a defined proposal and timeline before work begins.

What happens after launch?

The system is tested, documented, and handed off. Ongoing support can be scoped when the workflow needs monitoring or continued improvement.

How do you handle sensitive business data?

Access stays limited to the agreed need, credentials stay outside public code, and sensitive workflows receive a dedicated review during scoping.
