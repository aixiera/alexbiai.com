3. Inquiry forms, campaign data, and local storage

The inquiry form is delivered through FormSubmit. A submission may include the page, selected language, referring page, and available UTM campaign parameters so I can understand how the inquiry arrived. The site also provides a direct email option.

UTM parameters may be retained temporarily in session storage and attached to a later inquiry or booking link. Language and privacy choices are stored in local storage on your device. You can remove these values through browser controls.
