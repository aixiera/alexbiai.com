1. Information you choose to provide

If you use the inquiry form, email Kairui, or book a meeting, information may include your name, business name, email, optional phone number, website, industry, preferred contact method, and the business process you describe. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health information, or other sensitive information through the general inquiry form.

2. Inquiry form

When configured, the inquiry form is delivered through Formspree, a third-party form service. The submission also includes the page, language, referring page, and available campaign parameters so Kairui can understand where the inquiry came from. Formspree processes the submission under its own terms and privacy practices. If the hosted form is not configured or cannot send, the website offers an email fallback instead of silently discarding your message.

3. Booking

The booking page embeds and links to Google Appointment Schedule. Google may process information, cookies, and device data when the scheduler loads or when you book. The website can measure a visit to the booking page or an outbound scheduler click, but it does not claim to detect a completed booking inside the cross-origin Google interface.

4. Campaign parameters and local storage

If a link contains UTM campaign parameters, the site stores them temporarily in session storage and attaches them to a later inquiry or booking link. The language preference and privacy choices are stored in local storage so the site can remember them on the same device. These values can be cleared through your browser controls.

5. Optional analytics and advertising measurement

The site contains configuration points for Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, but no optional tracker loads unless its identifier has been configured and the related privacy choice has been accepted. Analytics and Marketing are separate choices. You may decline optional tracking and continue using the site, or reopen “Privacy choices” in the footer to change your selection.

6. Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process routine technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamps, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. Those providers operate under their own policies.

7. How information is used

  • to respond to inquiries and schedule conversations;
  • to evaluate whether a website or workflow project is a fit;
  • to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website;
  • to understand campaign performance when optional measurement is enabled and accepted;
  • to maintain reasonable business records and comply with applicable obligations.

8. Sharing and sale

Personal information is not sold or rented. It may be handled by service providers needed to host the site, deliver a form, provide scheduling, send email, or measure the site when configured. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably required by law, security, or the protection of legal rights.

9. Retention and security

Inquiry and correspondence records are kept only as long as reasonably useful for responding, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, or meeting applicable obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no website, email service, form provider, or booking system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices

You may decline optional tracking, request access to or correction of information you submitted, or ask that an inquiry record be deleted where appropriate. Contact bia446635@gmail.com. Identity may need to be verified before fulfilling a privacy request.

11. External links and changes

External demos, LinkedIn, Google, Formspree, SPIE, GitHub, and other linked services have their own practices. This policy may be updated when the website or its tools change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
