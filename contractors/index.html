FAQ

Do I need a new CRM?

Usually not. The first goal is to make your existing website, inbox, scheduling, CRM, or job software work together more clearly.

Can this work for emergency or after-hours calls?

The intake and alerting flow can distinguish urgency and route information, but it should support—not replace—your real emergency response process.

Not before measuring your current workflow. We first define what is being missed or repeated, then agree on practical measures for the project.
